About Naiwa & Our Team

The Story of Naiwa

Naiwa is a chubby yellow frog with creamy belly and big sleepy green eyes — a fan-made character inspired by the Nailong (Milk Dragon) meme universe. One day Naiwa looked at a cup of steamed milk, looked at the camera, and said nothing. That silence became our café.

This website is a student-built fan project for coursework only (non-commercial).
